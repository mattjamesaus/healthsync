Health Sync Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Health Sync is a personal iOS and iPadOS app that reads Apple Health data on the device and, after you connect a Google account, writes CSV exports into that account’s Google Drive.

What the app collects

Health Sync reads HealthKit samples you enable, such as workouts, activity, heart, and sleep. It also receives your Google account email, name, and user ID from Google Sign-In so the app can show which account is connected.

Where data goes

Health data stays on your device until you tap Connect Google Drive and complete Google’s consent screen. The app then writes monthly CSV files and a small JSON manifest into a HealthSync folder in your own My Drive. Health exports are sent to your Google Drive, not to a developer-operated export server. Diagnostic reporting is described below.

Google Drive access

The app requests only the drive.file scope. It can create the HealthSync folder and the files it writes there. It cannot list the rest of your Drive.

Disconnect and deletion

Disconnect revokes the Google grant and clears the local folder and file IDs. Deleting the app does not delete files already in Google Drive. Resetting the watermark only clears local dates; it does not delete Drive files.

Analytics and tracking

Health Sync does not use health data for advertising, marketing, or data mining. When the configured Sentry service is enabled, the app sends crash reports, errors and performance diagnostics to Sentry. Default personally identifying information is disabled, and the app removes Authorization headers and cookies from error requests and removes selected headers and request/response bodies from breadcrumbs. Health samples, CSV exports and Google tokens are not intentionally attached to diagnostic reports. The app does not track you across apps or websites.
